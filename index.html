yt-auto

yt-auto is a small personal automation tool. It creates short educational videos (script, voice-over, stock footage and captions) and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel on a daily schedule.

What the app does

  • Uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos to the channel of the person who owns and operates the tool.
  • Requests a single permission: uploading videos (youtube.upload).
  • Is used only by its owner. It is not offered to other users.
